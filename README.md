

TowerDefenseGame made for GDD335
No AI was used in the devlopment of TowerDefenseGame.
Made in Godot V4.6.2

All code and art are made by me. No music or sound effects are included this version.

Controls: 
Left mouse - place/select,
right mouse - move,
r - rotate,
middle mouse - cancel placement,

Players must defend a central building from enemy slimes.
